## 2. Monthly Tracking Scorecard Template

For each engine, record:
- **Mentioned (Yes/No):** Was Deez Prints cited or recommended?
- **Cited URL:** Which exact URL was referenced in footnotes or sources?
- **Factual Accuracy:** Were prices, fabric specs (240+ GSM, 100% cotton), and delivery terms (Karachi Rs. 200, nationwide Rs. 450) represented accurately?
- **Competitors Surfaced:** (e.g. Surteez, HavenWear, Dripzada).
- **Information Gaps:** Any missing attributes that prevented citation?
